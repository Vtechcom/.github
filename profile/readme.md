# Vtechcom – Hydra Engineering Team

Vtechcom is a development group based in Vietnam, focusing on **Hydra (Cardano Layer-2)** and high-performance Web3 applications.  
Our mission is simple: **make Hydra practical** by building SDKs, tools, platforms, and demos that help developers adopt Hydra easily.

---

## 🚀 Core Projects

### **🔹 Hydra SDK**
A comprehensive development kit for building Cardano & Hydra-enabled applications.  
Built with TypeScript, Turborepo monorepo architecture, WASM bindings, transaction builders, and Hydra Head management utilities.

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-sdk

---

### **🔹 Hydra Hub / Hydra Hexcore**
A “Hydra-Head-as-a-Service” orchestration platform with dashboards, admin UI, mapper service, and API gateway.  
Helps developers launch and manage multiple Hydra Heads with minimal effort.

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydrahub-portal-frontend

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-hub

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-hexcore-design-docs

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-hub-design-docs

---

### **🔹 Hydra One Docs**
Documentation and guidelines for Hydra One — defining recommended integration patterns for real-world applications.

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-one-docs

---

### **🔹 Hydra HTLC Demo**
A full working demo of **Hash Time Locked Contract (HTLC)** interactions on Hydra.  
Presented and highly praised by the Hydra Core Team during the November Monthly Review.

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-htlc-demo

---

### **🔹 Hydra SDK Technical Reports**
In-depth technical reports covering research notes, architectural decisions, R&D logs, and protocol exploration.

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-sdk-technical-reports

---

### **🔹 Hydra Perp (L2 Perpetual DEX)**
An experimental Perpetual Futures DEX running on Hydra L2, designed to deliver low-latency and high-throughput trading.

🔗 https://github.com/Vtechcom/hydra-perp
